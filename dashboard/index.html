    Method — what the numbers are, and what they are not

    Implied value = TVL × comps × growth × maturity × launch-probability × chain-spread × farm-discount. Growth uses the 7-day TVL change with diminishing returns after +80% and a sharper penalty for large drops. Maturity is 0.55 + 0.45·(1 − exp(−age/180d)). Launch probability is a logistic fit on the snapshot itself (token already trading vs not). No protocol is ranked from its name: every factor comes from the snapshot's own figures.

    Farm cost is an estimate, not snapshot data: each step's gas units × an assumed average gas price for its chain (see table). Net EV assumes 5% of implied value goes to an airdrop pool and the farmer's share equals farm capital / TVL, minus gas cost. Farm capital = $5,000 × (TVL/1M)^0.7, clamped [$2,000, $250,000].

    Honest limits: the offline fit ceiling for this feature family is about 0.47–0.49 Spearman — the board is a ranking claim, not a forecast, and it does not predict whether or when any token launches.
